--- layout: example.html title: WebGL Vector Layer Draw Order shortdesc: Overlapping circles stack according to their `z-index` docs: > The WebGL vector renderer honors the `z-index` style property (see [issue #16331](https://github.com/openlayers/openlayers/issues/16331)), so draw order is controlled by `z-index` rather than by the order of the style rules array. Here, circle "a" is styled by the first rule but has the highest `z-index` (3) and is drawn on top, "c" has `z-index` 2, and "b", styled by the second rule, has the lowest `z-index` (1) and is drawn at the bottom. experimental: true tags: "webgl, vector, z-index, style" ---
